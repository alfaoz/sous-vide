sous-vide

Cap how far your Mac's battery charges — slow long-term wear and keep it healthy. A tiny menu-bar app, on its own glass panel.

↓ Download for macOS
Apple Silicon · free & open source · source on GitHub

Charge limit + sailing

Hold at your limit and only resume charging after a band, killing micro-cycles.

Heat protection

Pause charging when the battery runs hot, with hysteresis so it doesn't flap.

Top up & discharge

One-shot charge to 100% before a trip, or actively drain to your limit while plugged in.

Calibration

Run a full calibration cycle on a schedule to keep the fuel gauge accurate.
